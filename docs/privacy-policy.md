# Privacy Policy — True Crime Pipeline

_Last updated: 29 July 2026_


## Who this covers

This application ("the app") is operated by Roman Halyuta as a personal
content-production tool. It prepares documentary-style videos and uploads them to
**the operator's own TikTok account**. It has no other users and offers no service
to the public.

## What data the app handles

- **TikTok access and refresh tokens** — issued after the operator signs in with
  TikTok. Stored only on the operator's local machine, in a file readable solely by
  that machine's user account. Never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's own API.
- **Basic account information** — nickname and posting settings returned by TikTok's
  `creator_info` endpoint. Displayed on screen during upload and not stored.
- **Video files** — produced locally by the app and uploaded to the operator's TikTok
  drafts.

The app collects no data from any other person, runs no analytics, sets no cookies
beyond a session identifier used during sign-in, and has no advertising.

## How the data is used

Solely to place the operator's own finished videos into the operator's own TikTok
drafts, from where the operator reviews and publishes them manually in the TikTok app.

## Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. The only external service
contacted with account data is TikTok itself.

## Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the local machine until the operator deletes them (removing
`data/tiktok_token.json`) or revokes access in TikTok's own settings
(Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions). Revoking access there
immediately ends the app's ability to act on the account.

## Security

Tokens are stored on a single, password-protected machine and are transmitted only
over HTTPS to TikTok's API.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and processes no data about them.
